# Textbook and references

The course does not follow one required textbook. A useful reference is Larry Wasserman, *All of Statistics: A Concise Course in Statistical Inference*.

Additional public notes:

- [Aditya Jaishankar — Fundamentals of Statistics](https://aditya-jaishankar.github.io/coursenotes/statistics/)
- [Jokerdii — Probability and Statistics](https://jokerdii.github.io/probability-and-statistics-notes/)
- [David Khachatrian — Fundamentals of Statistics notes](https://www.davidkhachatrian.com/assets/pdf/notes/Fundamentals%20of%20Statistics%20%2818.6501x%29.pdf)

These are external references. Check their licenses and attribution before copying material into this repository.
