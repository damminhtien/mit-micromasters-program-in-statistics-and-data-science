## Inference checklist

Before applying a method, identify the estimand, sampling model, parameter space, and what is observed. Then separate:

- the estimator as a function of data from the estimate obtained on one sample;
- finite-sample guarantees from asymptotic approximations;
- a confidence procedure from a posterior probability;
- a statistically detectable effect from a practically important effect.
