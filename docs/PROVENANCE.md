# Content provenance and licensing

This repository is a mixed study archive. A root MIT license does not automatically relicense material owned by MIT, edX, course instructors, publishers, or other contributors.

## Content categories

- **Personal notes:** summaries, explanations, notebooks, and reports written or assembled by the repository owner. These are offered under the repository license unless a file says otherwise.
- **Course material:** slides, PDFs, screenshots, and platform exports collected for study. Copyright and platform terms remain with their owners.
- **External references:** notes or links from other authors. Keep the original attribution and follow the source license.
- **Submitted work:** personal assignments and reports. They are examples of one learner's work, not official answers.
- **Showcase images:** the [showcase gallery](SHOWCASES.md) keeps owner-provided program letters, certificates, and artifact previews visible. Public copies use redacted derivatives for personal milestones; original source images remain local-only and are ignored by Git.

## Rules for additions

Every new imported asset should record its source, author or owner, course cohort, and access date where known. Do not add credentials, access tokens, private course data, or another person's personal information. Prefer linking to a source over copying a file when redistribution rights are unclear.

If a file's ownership or redistribution status is uncertain, open an issue and mark it for review instead of assuming that `LICENSE` covers it.
